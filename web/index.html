Agentic QR Payment

Pay at approved cafes and restaurants from your own USDC.inj vault, earn points, and turn them into ATOM, INJ or USDC.inj. ATOM always gives the most per point.

SIMULATION · NO ON-CHAIN TX
  1. Show a payment QR on the merchant POS
  2. Tap Pay in the app and confirm
  3. Turn your points into ATOM
Rewards are paused by the operator. Payments without points and withdrawals still work; your balance cannot be frozen.
